ACME CORPORATION
EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK

1. WORKING HOURS
Employees are expected to work 8 hours per day, Monday through Friday.
Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a one-hour lunch break.
Flexible work arrangements may be approved by the reporting manager.

2. ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE POLICY
Employees must inform their manager before taking leave.
Paid leave categories include:
- Casual Leave: 12 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Earned Leave: Accrued monthly

Unplanned absences exceeding 3 consecutive days require a medical certificate.

3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Remote work is permitted based on role and manager approval.
Employees working remotely must:
- Be available during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM)
- Maintain stable internet connectivity
- Attend mandatory meetings
- Ensure data confidentiality

The company reserves the right to revoke remote privileges if performance declines.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or misconduct will not be tolerated.
Confidential company information must not be shared externally.

Violations may lead to disciplinary action including termination.

5. IT AND SECURITY POLICY
Employees must use company-provided devices for official work.
Installing unauthorized software is prohibited.
Passwords must be kept confidential and changed every 90 days.
Phishing emails should be reported to the IT department immediately.

6. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year.
Evaluation criteria include:
- Work quality
- Team collaboration
- Initiative
- Attendance
- Goal achievement

Outstanding performers may receive bonuses or promotions.

7. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses will be reimbursed upon submission of valid receipts.
Reimbursable expenses include:
- Travel
- Accommodation
- Client meetings
- Approved office supplies

All claims must be submitted within 30 days.

8. TERMINATION POLICY
Employees must provide a 30-day notice before resignation.
The company may terminate employment with notice or payment in lieu.
Exit formalities must be completed before final settlement.

9. HEALTH AND SAFETY
Employees must follow workplace safety guidelines.
Emergency exits must remain unobstructed.
Incidents or hazards should be reported immediately.

10. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
Employees are encouraged to participate in training programs.
Mandatory compliance training must be completed annually.
Professional development courses may be sponsored based on role relevance.
